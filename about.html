About Me

Aspiring Researcher & Writer | Passionate about AI, Space and Defense Tech, and Geopolitics.

Who I Am

I'm Ananya—a lifelong learner with a restless mind and a steady pen. I'm an ECE student at Madan Mohan Malaviya University of Technology (MMMUT), Gorakhpur, originally from Faridabad, Haryana.

What Draws Me In

I'm drawn to the big, complex questions: how to align AI with human values, how to safeguard the planet, and how to design systems that help humanity flourish. My strengths lie in writing, interdisciplinary thinking, and a deep willingness to learn. From Python to political theory, I enjoy building bridges between ideas and disciplines.

My Background

Currently pursuing B.Tech in ECE (2024–2028) while building expertise in AI safety and governance research, geospatial analysis, and technical writing. I've done the CERN Solvay Programme and Yale's psychology course, and I speak Hindi and English fluently—with German and Italian in progress.

How I Work

I believe in combining technical depth with policy awareness. Understanding how AI systems work is only half the picture; grasping their broader strategic implications is the other half. I'm equally comfortable analysing transistor circuits as I am dissecting defense policy documents. Writing, for me, is how scattered late-night readings become structured ideas.
